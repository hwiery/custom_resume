AISM Pro (Internal) as a Head of Product Manager (2021)
AISM Pro is a real-time artificial intelligence composition system. Users can create music by simply visiting the website and selecting a few keywords. 
What I did -> To build an artificial intelligence composition system, I searched for papers, tested models, and tested parameters. To create data without copyright issues, I developed a tool to create and manage data by myself and created a framework that can be managed with a cloud concept. I conducted various studies on the appropriate amount of training and number of data, and created a generation routine with a rule base to secure genres and diversity. Finally, we studied the rendering system for converting the generated midi to audio format, and built a system for mixing and mastering by creating templates with presets and automatically reflecting them.
